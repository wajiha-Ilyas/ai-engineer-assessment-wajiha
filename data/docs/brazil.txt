Brazil

Brazil is the largest country in South America and the fifth-largest in the world by both area and population. It covers approximately 8.5 million square kilometers and has a population of about 215 million people. The capital is Brasília, while the largest city is São Paulo, one of the most populous cities in the Western Hemisphere.

Brazil is a federal presidential constitutional republic. The President serves as both head of state and head of government. The National Congress is the bicameral legislature, composed of the Federal Senate and the Chamber of Deputies.

The Brazilian economy is the ninth-largest in the world by nominal GDP, approximately $2.1 trillion. Key sectors include agriculture, mining, manufacturing, and services. Brazil is the world's largest exporter of coffee, soybeans, beef, and sugarcane ethanol. Petrobras is one of the world's largest oil companies and is headquartered in Rio de Janeiro.

Brazil is home to the Amazon rainforest, the world's largest tropical rainforest, covering over 5.5 million square kilometers. The Amazon River is the world's largest river by discharge volume. The rainforest is critical to global climate regulation and biodiversity.

Brazil is renowned for its culture, particularly football (soccer), Carnival, samba music, and bossa nova. The Brazilian national football team has won the FIFA World Cup a record five times (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002). Pelé, born Edson Arantes do Nascimento, is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time.

The Portuguese colonized Brazil starting in 1500, and Portuguese remains the official language. Brazil declared independence from Portugal on September 7, 1822.
