United States of America

The United States of America (USA) is a federal republic composed of 50 states, a federal district (Washington, D.C.), and several territories. It is located in North America, bordered by Canada to the north and Mexico to the south.

The US is the world's largest economy by nominal GDP, with a GDP of approximately $27 trillion as of 2024. Major industries include technology, finance, healthcare, defense, and entertainment. Silicon Valley in California is the global center of the technology industry, home to companies like Apple, Google, and Meta.

The US government consists of three branches: the legislative (Congress, made up of the Senate and House of Representatives), the executive (the President), and the judicial (Supreme Court).

The US population is approximately 335 million people, making it the third most populous country in the world. The capital is Washington, D.C., but the largest city is New York City, with a metropolitan population of over 20 million.

The United States was founded on July 4, 1776, when the Declaration of Independence was adopted, breaking away from British colonial rule. The Constitution was ratified in 1788 and remains the supreme law of the land.

Space exploration is a major achievement of the US. NASA, founded in 1958, led the Apollo program that landed humans on the Moon in 1969 during the Apollo 11 mission. Neil Armstrong was the first human to walk on the Moon.
