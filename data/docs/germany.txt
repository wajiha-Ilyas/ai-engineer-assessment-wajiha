Germany

Germany is a federal parliamentary republic located in Central Europe. It is the most populous member state of the European Union, with approximately 84 million inhabitants. The capital and largest city is Berlin.

Germany has the largest economy in Europe and the fourth-largest in the world by nominal GDP, approximately $4.4 trillion. It is a global leader in engineering, chemical manufacturing, and automotive production. Major companies include Volkswagen, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Siemens, and BASF.

The country is divided into 16 federal states (Bundesländer). The government consists of a Federal Chancellor (head of government) and a Federal President (ceremonial head of state). The parliament is the Bundestag, with an upper chamber called the Bundesrat representing the states.

Germany was the site of significant 20th-century history, including two World Wars and the Holocaust. After World War II, Germany was divided into West Germany (democratic) and East Germany (communist). The Berlin Wall, built in 1961, divided the city until it fell on November 9, 1989. Germany was reunified on October 3, 1990.

Germany is a founding member of the European Union and a key driver of European integration. It uses the euro as its currency. Germany is also a member of NATO and the G7.

Germany is globally recognized for its contributions to philosophy, music, and science. Notable Germans include Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Ludwig van Beethoven, Albert Einstein, and Karl Marx.
