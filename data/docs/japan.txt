Japan

Japan is an island nation located in East Asia, consisting of four main islands: Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku, along with thousands of smaller islands. Its capital and largest city is Tokyo, which is also the most populous metropolitan area in the world with over 37 million residents.

Japan has the third-largest economy in the world by nominal GDP, approximately $4.2 trillion. It is a global leader in manufacturing, particularly in automobiles and electronics. Major companies include Toyota, Sony, Honda, Panasonic, and Nintendo.

The country has a constitutional monarchy with a parliamentary government. The Emperor is the ceremonial head of state, while the Prime Minister is the head of government. The National Diet is Japan's bicameral legislature.

Japan has a population of approximately 125 million people. The country is known for its aging population and low birth rate, which poses long-term demographic challenges.

Japan's culture is rich and distinctive, combining ancient traditions with modern innovation. Sushi, ramen, anime, manga, and traditional arts like kabuki and tea ceremony are globally recognized aspects of Japanese culture.

Japan is located on the Pacific Ring of Fire and experiences frequent earthquakes. The devastating Tohoku earthquake of 2011 triggered a tsunami and the Fukushima nuclear disaster, one of the worst nuclear accidents in history.

Japan held the 2020 Summer Olympics (delayed to 2021 due to COVID-19) in Tokyo. The country has hosted the Olympics multiple times, including the 1964 Summer Olympics and the 1998 Winter Olympics in Nagano.
