Australia

Australia is a sovereign country comprising the mainland of the Australian continent, the island of Tasmania, and numerous smaller islands. It is the sixth-largest country by total area and has a population of approximately 26 million people. The capital is Canberra, while the largest city is Sydney.

Australia is a federal parliamentary constitutional monarchy. The monarch of Australia (currently King Charles III) is the head of state, represented by the Governor-General. The Prime Minister is the head of government. The Parliament of Australia is bicameral, consisting of the Senate and the House of Representatives.

Australia has a highly developed market economy, with a GDP of approximately $1.7 trillion. Major industries include mining, agriculture, financial services, and tourism. Australia is the world's largest exporter of iron ore, coal, and lithium. The country has significant reserves of gold, natural gas, and uranium.

Australia is famous for its unique wildlife, including kangaroos, koalas, wombats, echidnas, and platypuses. The Great Barrier Reef, off the northeastern coast, is the world's largest coral reef system and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Australia was colonized by Britain starting in 1788, with the establishment of a penal colony at Sydney Cove. The country federated on January 1, 1901, forming the Commonwealth of Australia.

Australia has hosted the Summer Olympics twice: in Melbourne in 1956 and in Sydney in 2000. The Sydney 2000 Olympics are widely regarded as one of the best-organized Games in Olympic history.

Cricket, Australian rules football, and rugby are among the most popular sports in Australia. The Australian Open is one of the four Grand Slam tennis tournaments and is held annually in Melbourne.
